When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most single-fixture clogs can wait a few hours. These situations cannot, so call the 24/7 line first and work through the list while you wait.

  • ✓Water is coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain when you run the washer or flush a toilet. That points to a blockage in the main drain line, not in one fixture.
  • ✓Several fixtures are slow or gurgling at the same time, and the lowest drain in the house is the worst.
  • ✓Sewage smell or dark water is rising in a basement or crawlspace. Keep children and pets out of the area and do not run any water.
  • ✓Stop using sinks, toilets, the dishwasher and the washing machine until a plumber has cleared the line. Every gallon you send down has nowhere to go.
  • ✓If water is touching an outlet, a furnace or a water heater burner, shut off power to that area at the panel only if the floor around the panel is dry.

If you are on a septic system, add one more rule: do not pump the tank on your own to relieve a backup before the blockage is located. A tank can be fine while the pipe between it and the house is the problem.

What clogged drains in Wareham usually turn out to be

Wareham's own housing plan puts the median home at about 1969 construction, and roughly one in five homes dates from 1939 or earlier. Older drain lines tend to be cast iron or clay inside, with rough interior walls that catch grease and paper long before a newer plastic pipe would.

Wareham also has a large seasonal housing stock, from cottages around Onset to summer streets near the water. A cottage that sat closed for months can have dried-out trap seals, a sediment film in the lines and a first clog on the first busy weekend of the season.

Roots are the other repeat cause. Tree roots follow moisture into joints in a buried line and thicken until paper catches on them. When that is the cause, snaking opens the pipe for a while, and the right next step is a camera look and, if needed, hydro jetting to scour the pipe wall.

Symptoms that tell us where the clog is

How the house behaves tells a plumber where to start before any tool comes out. Describe these on the phone and we can plan the right cable and the right time slot.

One slow sink

The clog is usually in that fixture's trap or its short branch line. A hand auger or small cable clears it in a single visit.

Tub gurgles when the toilet flushes

Air is being pushed through a partly blocked branch or the main line. This is the early warning before a full backup.

Basement floor drain bubbles

The lowest opening in the house shows a main-line blockage first. Treat it as urgent and stop using water.

Slow drains after every storm

Groundwater or roots entering a cracked line, or a municipal main surcharging. A camera inspection sorts out which one.

Snaking, cabling and when we step up to jetting

A drain snake is a flexible steel cable with a head shaped to the job. For a sink or tub clog we use a small drum machine that cuts through hair, soap scum and food. For the main line we run a heavier cable with a cutting head that can open a root mass.

Cabling has a limit. A cable pokes a hole through a blockage, but it does not scrub the pipe wall. If grease or scale keeps rebuilding, hydro jetting pushes high-pressure water through the line and removes it, and a sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe is cracked, bellied or offset.

We will say plainly which tool fits your drain and why, and we will not sell jetting for a simple trap clog.

Kitchen sink clogs start with grease

Kitchen drains are among the most common calls we hear about, and they are mostly grease. Warm grease runs down as a liquid, cools in the first few feet of pipe and layers up with food solids until the opening is the size of a pencil.

Garbage disposals do not change this. A disposal grinds food into smaller pieces, but the pieces still settle in the horizontal run. If you have a disposal that hums or leaks along with the clog, garbage disposal repair is a separate fix we can handle in the same visit.

On a septic system, kitchen habits matter even more. Grease that reaches the tank can end up in the leach field, so scraping pans into the trash before washing them protects both the drain and the system.

Town sewer or septic? It changes the advice

Wareham's sewer department runs about 61 miles of gravity pipe and 45 pump stations, and the town's housing plan notes that areas north of Route 25 could not be added to the sewer system. If your home is in the sewered part of town, a blockage can be in your own lateral or in the town main, and the first question is which side of the property line it is on.

If your home has a septic system, a clog that will not clear is also a reason to think about the tank, the distribution box and the leach field. Massachusetts requires a Title 5 inspection when a septic property is sold, so recurring backups before a sale are worth sorting out early.

Permits and drain cleaning

Under 248 CMR 3.05(1)(c), clearing a stoppage does not need a plumbing permit. That is why a clogged drain can be cabled the same day, while replacing a broken drain section is permit work that a licensed plumber arranges with the Wareham Inspectional Services Department.
