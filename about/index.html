What we are, and what we are not

We answer the phone and send the right plumber. The plumbing work is done by licensed and insured plumbers in our network, who come to your home and give you a price before they start.

We do not ask you to create an account or wait for an email reply. If something is leaking, a phone call is faster, so that is the only way to reach us for service.

How a call works

Every call follows the same short path, whether it is 2 p.m. or 2 a.m.

  1. 1

    You call the line

    A dispatcher answers and asks for your address, what is happening and whether water or sewage is still flowing.

  2. 2

    Safety first

    If water is running, the dispatcher walks you through closing the main valve and staying clear of wet electrical areas.

  3. 3

    A plumber is matched

    Urgent problems are sent out right away. Problems that can wait are booked as same-day appointments when available.

  4. 4

    Price before work

    The plumber looks at the problem, explains the options and gives a clear price before work starts.

  5. 5

    Permit and follow-up

    If the job needs a permit under 248 CMR 3.00, the licensed plumber handles it with the Wareham Inspectional Services Department.

How to verify any plumber's license

You should never have to take a plumber's word for it. Massachusetts licenses plumbers and gas fitters through the Division of Professional Licensure, and the lookup is free and public.

  • 1Go to mass.gov and search for Check an Occupational Board License (eLIPSE).
  • 2Choose the Plumbers and Gas Fitters board.
  • 3Enter the plumber's name or license number, which they should give you on request.
  • 4Confirm that the license is current and matches the person who shows up.

This works for us and for any other company you consider. If a plumber cannot or will not give you a license number, that tells you something.

What a dispatch model means for you

A single company has a fixed number of trucks and a fixed schedule. A network of licensed plumbers lets the dispatcher look for whoever is open nearest your address, which is how same-day appointments become possible when a slot is available.

The trade-off is that the plumber who arrives may not be the one who came last time. That is why every job ends with a clear price agreed before work, and why we point you to the state license lookup instead of asking you to trust a brand name.

What we will not claim

You will not find review counts, star ratings or years in business on this site, because we would rather leave a gap than print a number we cannot prove. The same goes for awards and certifications.

What you can check yourself is the license, the permit and the price. Under 248 CMR 3.05 a permit has to be issued to a properly licensed individual before most plumbing work starts, so ask to see that it has been pulled for any larger job, whoever you hire.
