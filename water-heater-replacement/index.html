When a failed water heater is an emergency

A heater that simply makes lukewarm water can wait for a daytime visit. These situations cannot, and they are the reason people call the after-hours line.

  • ✓Water is pooling around the base of the tank, not dripping from a fitting above it. A leak from the tank shell cannot be patched, and the tank can let go further.
  • ✓The tank sits above finished space, such as a second-floor closet or an attic platform, and the drain pan is full or missing.
  • ✓A gas heater smells like gas. Leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first, then call us once you are outside.
  • ✓The relief valve is discharging steadily or the pipes bang and the tank rumbles. That points to overheating or runaway pressure.
  • ✓There is no hot water at all and the house has infants, elderly residents or anyone who needs hot water for care.

Shutting off the water supply to a leaking tank usually stops the flood within a minute. Leave the hot outlet pipe alone, since it may still be hot enough to scald.

Repair the tank or replace it?

A tank that is leaking from its own steel shell is finished. Rust-through happens when the anode rod, the sacrificial metal inside, is used up and the glass lining cracks. No part swap fixes that, which is why this page is about replacement and water heater repair covers the failures a part can solve.

Age is the second signal. Tanks that are into their second decade, rumble when they fire, or have already needed an element or gas valve are usually near the end. Paying for a fourth repair on a tank that will fail anyway is the choice we try to steer people away from.

If the heater is young and the leak turns out to be a fitting or the relief valve, we say so and repair it instead. You should never buy a tank you did not need.

What happens during a replacement visit

A standard like-for-like swap is usually a single visit. The sequence below is what a licensed plumber does on site.

  1. 1

    Shut down and drain

    Gas or power is turned off, the cold supply is closed and the old tank is drained through a hose to a floor drain or outside.

  2. 2

    Disconnect and remove

    Water lines, the gas connection or electric feed, and the vent or flue are disconnected. The old tank goes out of the house.

  3. 3

    Set the new tank

    The new heater goes on a drain pan where code and space allow, with new flexible connectors, a fresh relief valve and a discharge pipe run to a safe spot.

  4. 4

    Vent and connect

    Gas units get a proper vent connection and a leak test of the gas joints with approved solution. Electric units get a checked, correctly sized circuit.

  5. 5

    Refill, purge and test

    The tank fills with a hot tap open so air escapes. The plumber checks for leaks, sets temperature and fires the unit.

  6. 6

    Permit and sign-off

    The plumbing permit is pulled by the licensed plumber and the inspection is arranged through the town, as described below.

Permit rules in Massachusetts

Replacing a water heater needs a plumbing permit in Massachusetts, and 248 CMR 3.05 says that permit must be pulled by a licensed plumber, not by an apprentice or a homeowner on the contractor's behalf. The application is made before the work begins, and a permit lapses if the work does not start within 90 days.

In Wareham the permit and timing questions go through the Inspectional Services plumbing inspector at Memorial Town Hall on Marion Road, which also runs an online permit portal. For a true emergency, the same regulation lets the licensee install a replacement first if tests are done and the inspector is notified, with inspection by the next working day.

What drives the price of a new tank

Fuel and typeElectric, natural gas and propane tanks differ, and a power-vent or condensing unit is a bigger job than an atmospheric one.
Tank sizeA larger family needs a larger tank. We size by household use, not by what the old label said.
Venting and gas pipingAn old flue that does not meet the new unit, or a gas line that needs altering, adds labor and parts.
Location and accessA basement with stairs is routine. A tight closet, crawlspace or second floor takes more time and more hands.

Choosing the replacement type

The unit that failed is not always the right one to buy again. A few honest points on the main choices for a Wareham home, and our tankless water heater page goes deeper on that option.

Like-for-like tank

  • Fastest swap, often the same day
  • Uses the existing vent, gas line or circuit
  • Lowest disruption when the old unit worked well
  • Good for rentals and seasonal cottages

Step up or switch

  • A heat pump or hybrid unit can cut electric use but needs space, clearance and a drain
  • Tankless units free floor area but need the right gas size or heavy electric service
  • Condensing gas models need a drain for acidic condensate
  • Any switch takes more planning, so it suits a planned visit, not a flooded floor

Wareham homes and what the old tank tells us

Wareham's housing plan puts the median home at about 1969 and notes a large seasonal stock. A cottage heater that sits through a long closed season can corrode from the outside in, because a cold, damp utility space and salt air attack the jacket, the vent and the fittings. A shutdown cottage that was drained badly in fall can also leave a heater cracked by freeze before anyone sees it.

Two separate water districts serve the town, and homes in Carver next door mostly run on private wells, so the water feeding a tank varies a lot across our service area. That matters because the water going in sets how fast an anode rod is used up. When we replace a tank we check the old anode and the sediment layer, since both show how that supply has been treating the steel.

After the new tank is in

Set the thermostat low enough to avoid scalds while still holding the temperature you need, and ask the plumber to show you the shutoff valves and the relief valve. Lift the relief lever once a year so it does not seize.

If the old tank failed early, have the line checked for pressure and consider a planned install review on the next property. If you rent the unit out, a tank on a pan with a drain run is cheap insurance against another flood.

What to do when the tank fails

A few steps reduce damage and risk, and they apply whether or not a plumber is on the way.

  • ✓Close the cold water valve at the top of the tank and shut off the breaker or gas control.
  • ✓Open a hot tap upstairs so the tank can drain by gravity without suction, and protect the floor with towels.
  • ✓Do not attempt to relight a gas pilot on a leaking tank, and leave the house if you smell gas.
  • ✓Photograph the data label on the old tank, which tells the plumber the size and fuel.
