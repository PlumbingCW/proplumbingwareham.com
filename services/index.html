How to choose the right service page

Start with the symptom you can see. Water on the floor from a pipe points to burst pipe repair, a toilet or tub that gurgles and backs up points to sewer line cleaning, and a tank that leaks at the base points to water heater replacement.

If you cannot tell, call and describe the sound, the smell and where the water shows up. The dispatcher will match the call to the right type of plumber and tell you whether it needs to go out tonight or can wait for a same-day slot.

Before you call about any service

  • ✓Note where the water or smell is strongest, and whether it comes and goes.
  • ✓Find your main shutoff valve, or the pressure tank shutoff if you have a private well.
  • ✓Know whether your home is on town sewer or a septic system.
  • ✓Take a photo of any label on a water heater or boiler.

That information lets the dispatcher send a plumber with the right parts. Our about page explains how a call works from start to finish.
