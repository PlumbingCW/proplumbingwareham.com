Towns we serve beyond Wareham

Water source, sewer or septic, and house age all change how a plumbing problem is handled. That is why every town below has its own page rather than a shared template.

Carver, MA

Carver, ZIPs 02330 and 02355. Wells, septic and cranberry country, where a failed pressure tank can look like a burst pipe. See emergency plumbing in Carver.

Marion, MA

Marion, ZIP 02738. Older coastal houses on Buzzards Bay with aging supply lines. Read about plumbers serving Marion.

Bourne, MA

Bourne, ZIPs 02532, 02534, 02559 and 02562. Seasonal homes and village streets across the canal. See plumbing help in Bourne.

Rochester, MA

Rochester, ZIP 02770. Rural lots, private wells and septic systems. See plumbing service in Rochester.

Middleborough, MA

Middleborough, ZIP 02346. A mix of town water, sewered streets and outlying wells. See plumbers in Middleborough.

How coverage works

A call to the 24/7 line is routed to a licensed plumber who can reach your address. Inside Wareham the ZIP codes are 02571 for Wareham Center, 02558 for Onset, 02576 for West Wareham and 02538 for East Wareham.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. Coverage depends on the address and the time of day, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether a plumber can get there.

What to have ready when you call

  • ✓The street address, including the village if you know it.
  • ✓Whether you are on town water or a private well.
  • ✓Whether the house uses sewer or a septic system.
  • ✓What you can already see, hear or smell.
