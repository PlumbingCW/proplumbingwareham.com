Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Wareham Pro Plumbing collects when you call us or visit this website, and how we use it.

Speak with a plumber
(508) 389-4045
☎ Call now

Information we collect

When you call our line we may collect your name, phone number, service address and a description of your plumbing problem. We use this information to dispatch a plumber and to follow up on your request.

When you visit this website, your browser sends basic technical information such as your IP address and device type to our hosting provider.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or end the call.

How we use information

We use call information to send a licensed plumber to your address, to answer questions about your service and to improve how our line works. We share your name, address and problem details with the plumber assigned to your job.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address when the page loads and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

We keep call records only as long as needed for service, quality review and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by emailing contact@proplumbingwareham.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.
