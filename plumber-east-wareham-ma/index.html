Plumber in East Wareham, MA

East Wareham, ZIP code 02538, is where Route 6 and Route 28 meet. Homes, apartments and businesses share the same roads, and that blend is the reason this page is split between residential and commercial plumbing questions.

Be aware that 02538 is also used by other parts of Wareham, including White Island Shores and part of Onset. If your neighborhood has its own name, check the pages for those areas as well.

A crossroads, not a single neighborhood

The 1981 survey from the Massachusetts Historical Commission notes the Sandwich Railroad of 1847 and the Route 6 bridge over the Head of the Bay, built in 1935. It also records a cranberry processing factory on Sandwich Road that by then was part of a commercial strip, and its description is dated.

The town's 2022 housing plan says an affordable housing project of 150 units had been approved for East Wareham. That was an approval, not a completed building, so check the current status before relying on it.

What this means for a plumbing call: you may live in a single house, a condo, an apartment or a unit above a business. The right first step is different for each.

Which kind of property are you calling about?

The first question on the phone is usually the type of property. These are the usual answers and what they lead to.

PropertyWho usually owns the plumbingWhat to tell the plumber
Single-family houseThe homeowner, from the meter or the sewer connection inwardWhere the problem is and what changed
Condo unitThe unit owner inside the walls, the association for shared linesWhether the problem is in your unit or a shared pipe
Rental apartmentThe landlord, with the lease setting who callsTell the landlord first and keep it in writing
Shop or officeThe business owner or the building owner, per the leaseFixture count, hours and whether the water is off

Homes and businesses: what changes

The tools are the same. The questions are not.

A home

  • The call is about comfort and damage control.
  • A permit is needed for a replaced heater or new fixtures.
  • Insurance paperwork often comes next.

A business

  • The call is also about opening hours and customers.
  • The lease may decide who authorizes work.
  • Fixtures and grease traps can have extra rules.

Permits and licenses in Wareham

Plumbing work needs a permit under 248 CMR 3.05 before it starts, with exceptions for clearing a stoppage and for leak repairs inside a faucet or valve. The permit is issued to a licensed individual through Wareham Inspectional Services.

Check any plumber in the state's Check a License tool before work begins.

Source type: 248 CMR 3.00 and Wareham Inspectional Services
