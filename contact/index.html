What happens when you call

Have this information ready and the call will move quickly.

  1. 1

    Give your address

    Include the town or village, such as Onset or West Wareham, so the dispatcher can confirm coverage.

  2. 2

    Describe the problem

    Say what you see, hear and smell, and whether water is still running.

  3. 3

    Follow the safety steps

    The dispatcher will explain how to close the main valve and stay away from wet electrical areas.

  4. 4

    Get a plumber on the way

    Urgent problems go out right away, and others are booked same-day when a slot is open.
